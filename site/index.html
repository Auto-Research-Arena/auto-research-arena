How overall Elo is calculated

Each pair of methods is compared on the final target values of its best run. A lower value wins; equal values tie. Every included target receives the same nominal weight: 1/9 across all nine targets. Missing targets are excluded.

Training time is rounded to the nearest 30-second checkpoint with 30 × floor(seconds / 30 + 0.5) for ranking, tables and curves. Matching checkpoints tie; other targets retain their task’s tiebreaks. Downloaded submission JSON preserves raw measurements. Only selected endpoints enter Elo, without adjustments for search budget or reference scores.

A comparison’s update reflects its proportional improvement: the absolute log ratio is divided by the target’s median nonzero pairwise log gap, transformed with log₂(1 + gap), and capped at 3. Exact ties make no update.

Ratings start at 1500 and use standard Elo updates with K = 32 and a 400-point scale. We average 4,000 shuffled comparison orders with seed 20260902. Equal and opposite updates keep the cohort mean at 1500; there is no fixed maximum. Download full-precision ratings and calculation settings. The exported match-order standard deviation describes sensitivity to comparison order, not experimental uncertainty.
